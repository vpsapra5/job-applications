The opportunity   The Service Delivery Center is made up of high-performing, U.S.-based professionals who work closely with EY engagement teams to deliver project-based work and managed services to government clients. The Service Delivery Center is a core component of the Government and Infrastructure practice.  We currently have an opportunity for a Data, Application & Automation Delivery Senior to join our growing AI & Data practice. In this role, you will build, maintain, and enhance the data platforms, databases, applications, codebases, reports, integrations, and automations that support client operations.  You will independently own defined features or technical workstreams and contribute throughout the solution lifecycle, from requirements and design through development, testing, release, maintenance, and continuous improvement. You will work with EY engagement teams and client stakeholders to deliver reliable solutions, resolve complex issues, and improve the quality and efficiency of established systems and processes.
