It employs a ground-breaking focus with development responsibilities for customer-facing capabilities that deepen and expand digital engagement, as well as core technical capabilities that cut across business lines and customer segments.   American Express Ads, Offers, and Dining Technology brings together our advertising, offers, and dining platforms to elevate the Membership experience. As part of Team Amex, you’ll experience a culture built on innovation, shared values, and an unwavering commitment to back our customers and colleagues. You’ll have the support, flexibility, and autonomy to make an impact while helping shape the future of how people discover, book, and enjoy dining experiences worldwide.   Responsibilities      Lead the design, development, and implementation of scalable data pipelines and data architecture to support dining analytics, reporting, and downstream business applications.   Build and maintain reliable batch and incremental ETL/ELT workflows, including ingesting Rooam POS data from AWS S3 into Snowflake and transforming it into analytics-ready datasets.   Design and optimize Snowflake data models, including staging, curated, and business-ready data layers to support reporting, analytics, and operational use cases.   Develop scalable data processing solutions using Python, SQL, and AWS cloud services while ensuring performance, reliability, and maintainability.   Implement data quality validation, reconciliation, monitoring, and alerting to ensure the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of Rooam POS data.   Partner with Product, Business, Analytics, Architecture, and Engineering teams to define data requirements and deliver scalable data solutions.   Drive performance optimization across Snowflake by tuning SQL queries, optimizing storage strategies, and managing compute resources efficiently.   Lead technical design discussions, mentor engineers, and promote engineering best practices across data engineering initiatives.   Strengthen platform reliability through automation, CI/CD, observability, documentation, and operational excellence.   Support enterprise data governance by maintaining metadata, documentation, lineage, and secure access to data assets.     Qualifications      Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field.   8+ years of experience in Data Engineering, Software Engineering, or Enterprise Data Platforms.   Strong hands-on expertise in:   Snowflake   SQL and advanced query optimization   Python   ETL/ELT pipeline development and orchestration   Experience designing and implementing scalable data pipelines and cloud-based data integration solutions.   Strong understanding of data warehousing concepts, dimensional modeling, and modern data lake/lakehouse architectures.   Experience implementing data quality frameworks, reconciliation processes, schema evolution handling, and production monitoring.   Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, Git, Infrastructure as Code, and automated deployment practices.   Excellent problem-solving, communication, stakeholder management, and cross-functional collaboration skills.   Proven ability to lead end-to-end delivery of enterprise-scale data engineering initiatives across multiple teams.
